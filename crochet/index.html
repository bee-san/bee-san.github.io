    How to write a pattern
    • One round per line: R3: (sc, inc)*6 (18). Rnd and Round work too.
    • Ranges repeat a round: R6-9: sc*24 (24).
    • 2sc means 1 sc in each of the next 2 holes. inc*6, 6 inc and inc x6 all mean 6 increases.
    • Repeat brackets with *6, x6, ×6 or 6 times.
    • sc around is 1 sc in every hole. 6 sc in magic ring or sc*6 in MR starts a piece.
    • The count at the end is checked: (18), [18] or = 18.
    • A line ending in : or starting with # starts a new piece, like Body: or # Arms.
    • Any other line is kept as a note on the round above it.

    Glossary

    sc
    single crochet: 1 stitch in 1 hole
    inc
    increase: 2 sc in the same hole (2 taps here)
    2sc
    1 sc in each of the next 2 holes
    dec
    decrease: work 2 holes together into 1 stitch
    ( ) × 6
    do what's in the brackets 6 times
    = 24
    you should have 24 stitches at the end of the round
    hole
    the top of a stitch (the V) in the round below
